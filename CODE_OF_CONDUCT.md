# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Open Memory Protocol project
a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive;
- accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users; and
- showing empathy toward other participants.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, unwelcome private
contact, and publishing private or sensitive information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not
publish sensitive details in an issue. Security reports follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers may remove or edit comments, reject contributions, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
